Miata ECU: parts JLCPCB won't fit

Everything you buy and solder yourself for the daughterboard rev 0.5 and the five motherboards (1989-93 and 1994-95 rev 0.6, 1996-97 / 1999-2000 / 2001-05 rev 0.4). Set how many of each you're building and the quantities update. Tick items off as they arrive.

Quantities are per board or per car times your counts above; buy a few spare crimp terminals and shunts. The JLCPCB parts lists (fab/jlcpcb_bom.csv) now hold only the SMD parts and USB-C, so nothing here is double-ordered. The same list is in each board’s fab/self_order_bom.csv, and docs/order_all_boards.csv adds up the whole build in a format LCSC’s BOM tool reads. Part numbers come from the boards' BOMs and the READMEs in v3ai/miata-ecus; check stock on the day you order.
