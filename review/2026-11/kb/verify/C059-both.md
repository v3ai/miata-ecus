SD-write peaks are transient, not steady state, so 146/157 C is not a steady-state figure. 5.0 V input is nominal; 0.35 A x 1.7 V is the upper bound only.
Also: 125 C is an operating rating, 150 C the abs max; the 126 C case exceeds the rating by about 1 K at a hot-case assumption (85 C air inside case), with 24 K margin to abs max.
