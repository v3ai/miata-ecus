# C007 consequence check - verdict: PARTLY (severity high -> medium; mostly already documented)

Checked:
- review brief s6 already lists "injector inductance unknown (8 vs 20 mH)", "ordered rev 0.9 ... documented temperatures assumed 8 mH". repo/VERIFICATION.md N8 (line ~532) already says sequential drivers absorb their own turn-off energy (6 mJ, 0.36 W at 8 mH), reach shutdown at 90% duty / 7200 rpm, "Proposed, not made: a TVS on each injector output ... Measure the injectors' inductance before deciding". Rev 0.10 adds clamps (D42-49) - so the design intent and the gap on ordered boards are known; only the 20 mH numbers are new.
- Arithmetic (P09 note, rechecked): 20 mH, 14.4 V / 14 ohm -> 1.03 A; stored 10.6 mJ; driver clamp 45 V takes E*45/(45-14.4) = ~15.6 mJ/turn-off; sequential 7200 rpm = 60 injections/s/injector -> 0.93 W -> +44 K at the repo's 48 K/W (a copper-area fit, not measured), plus board rise 23-31 K. Matches the claim. At 8 mH: 0.37 W, +18 K - harmless.
- Driver energy is far below Eas (200 mJ single pulse) per event; the only issue is average heating. ST Fig 32 repetitive 125 C curve 1.77 A at 20 mH vs 1.03 A: inside.
- Claimed "over shutdown at 85 C air": tool adds no ambient; 85 C + 78-86 K = 163-171 C, arithmetic OK. But it needs all of: 20 mH injectors (no measurement exists; only one other 14.8 ohm injector fit at 20.3 mH), 85 C air inside the stock ECU case (cabin/under-seat, realistic hot-day max maybe 50-60 C), sustained 7200 rpm at ~80% duty (track/dyno only). Thermal shutdown of a self-protected driver is a thermal-cycling output dropout (lean/misfire), not damage; the part is protected.
- At street duty, e.g. 4000 rpm, 3 ms pulses: dissipation scales with rpm (0.5 W, +24 K) -> fine even at 20 mH.
- Affects ORDERED boards: yes (ordered db lacks D42-D49; 1994-2005 sequential depends on driver clamps). 1989-93 is separately handled by mb D3/D4.

Corrected statement: On the ordered rev 0.9 board each sequential injector driver absorbs its turn-off energy. If injectors are ~20 mH this is ~0.9 W/driver at sustained 7200 rpm (+44 K driver-over-board, ~+80 K over air per repo thermal tool); only in a hot-case combination (hot case air + sustained redline) does it reach thermal shutdown (self-protected, not destructive). At <=8 mH it is a non-issue. Already documented in substance (injector L unknown; N8 proposal); not new apart from the 20 mH numbers. Severity medium/low for street use, high only for track/dyno at redline.

Fix: cheapest first - measure one injector with an LCR meter (<8 mH: do nothing). Else per-injector TVS (1.5SMC16A / SMCJ16A-class, ~16 V) from each injector wire to VBAT at harness/header side by hand (4 parts for sequential cars; 1989-93 motherboard already has D3/D4), or just fit a cooling/duty note. The suggested fix is sound (clamp voltage 16.8 max + 14.4-16 V stays below VCLTH 36 V min); "limit sustained rpm" is an adequate stop-gap. Not verified: actual injector L, true board thermal resistance.
