# C046 verdict: partly (downgrade to low)
Checked: design.py:433-446 (R61/R63 4.7k series, R62/R64 1k pull-up from V5_EXT via D35, 1n, D13/D14 clamps) matches the claim. Ordered rev 0.9 daughterboard has the same R61-R64 values and nets (evidence/ord_db pcb_footprints.csv, pcb_nets.txt: IN_HALL1 = J2.13 R61.1 R62.2), so the circuit is on the ordered boards.
Arithmetic: (5-0.3)/1k = 4.7 mA is right, but the 4.7k series resistor sits between the pull-up node and the MCU pin, so the sensor sinks ~4.7 mA at Vol while the pin sees only Vol*4.7/5.7 (a divider). At the pin the VIL margin is therefore larger than the claim implies: even Vol = 1.0 V gives about 0.8 V at the pin (below VIL 0.99 V). Hall open-collector outputs are normally rated 20 mA or more with Vol about 0.2-0.4 V, so 4.7 mA is unremarkable. Not shown that the Mazda CAS/CMP cannot do it; neither datasheet exists (repo/research/pinouts_9605.md only says Hall/open-collector, needs pull-up; Hellen uses 4.7k). Speeduino comparison and forum noise report (R03.02) is anecdotal; root cause not proven (per claim itself); I did not re-read the forum thread.
(a) Not listed in review brief section 6; VERIFICATION.md has no sink-current item (only clamps/short-to-battery for the hall inputs). Not documented, but low value.
(b) Affects ordered boards in principle (same parts), but only as a bench test; R62/R64 are 0603, hand-swap to 2.2k-4.7k is easy.
Corrected statement: Hall pull-up sink of ~4.7 mA is within normal open-collector Hall capability; the sensor Vol at that current is unmeasured (low risk). Field noise on hall inputs is a reported but unproven issue on similar boards. Severity low.
Fix: on first bench run measure Vol and edge shape at the CAS/CMP; optionally log tooth noise. No board change needed; optional unpopulated Schmitt footprint only for the current design.
