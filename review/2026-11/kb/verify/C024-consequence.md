# C024 consequence check: international shipping cost, import VAT/DDP
Verdict: partly. Facts plausible, severity overstated, not a board issue.
Checked: USPS Postal Bulletin 22707 (July 2026) exists; DDP effective 2026-07-01 on PMEI/PMI/FCPIS (web search, corroborates R26.06; country list and per-country status not re-verified, sources themselves differ in wording). Rate tables (R26.04) not independently re-verified (cannot reach Notice 123 prices); treat as reported. R26.07 is "inferred", medium confidence, fetches of EU/AU sources blocked.
Consequence: this is a store-policy item. If the seller charges shipping at checkout (Shopify default is calculated or flat), the ~$26-87 is paid by the buyer and costs the owner nothing. Real risk is only (a) absorbing shipping on a $399 item, (b) buyer surprised by border VAT/fees and refusing delivery or disputing. Neither affects hardware, boards or the ordered boards. Not documented in the repo (grep of website/, README, VERIFICATION found no international policy). "high" is too high; medium/low is right.
Fix: state "international buyers pay shipping and import taxes" on the Shipping page; charge calculated shipping with insured PMI (~$61 Canada, ~$87 UK reported); use DDP where required (FR, DE etc.). Cheaper alternative: ship US/Canada only at launch.
