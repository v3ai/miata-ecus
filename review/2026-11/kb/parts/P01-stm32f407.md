## 2. Datasheet digest (DS8626 Rev 9)
- Abs max (Table 11/12): VDD-VSS -0.3..4.0 V; FT pin VSS-0.3..VDD+4; other pins max 4.0; injection FT -5/+0 mA (positive injection not possible), other +-5 mA, sum +-25 mA; IIO 25 mA, IVDD 240 mA; TJ max 125 (Table 13), 105 C for suffix 6 (Table 14).
- Operating (Table 14): VDD 1.8-3.6; VDDA 2.4-3.6 for full ADC, must equal VDD (<=300 mV difference during ramps); VREF+ : VDDA-VREF+ < 1.2 V; FT/RST input to 5.5 V (VDD>=2 V) but not in analog mode or oscillator mode; TTa pins limit VDDA+0.3. PA4 and PA5 are TTa (Table 9), all other analog pins used here (PA1-3, PA6, PA7, PC4, PC5, PB0, PB1) are FT.
- PC13-PC15: supplied via power switch, 3 mA total, <=2 MHz, 30 pF, not for LED drive (Table 9 note 3).
- VCAP (Table 16): 2.2 uF each, ESR < 2 ohm. HSE (Table 32): Gm >=5 mA/V, Gm_crit_max 1 mA/V, CL 5-25 pF recommended, 10 pF rough stray.
- BOR levels: L1 falling about 2.1 V, L3 2.83 V (Table 19). Supply current Table 20 above. ThetaJA LQFP100 43 C/W (Table 98).
- Pin table for LQFP100: see repo/scripts/stm32f407v_lqfp100_pins.txt (agrees with ST XML on all 100 pins per check_mcu_pins.py).

### 3b. Footprint
LQFP-100 14x14 0.5: pads 1.6x0.3, centre span 15.35 mm (x 153.325..168.675), pin1 at (153.325, 92.0) top-left, counter-clockwise. Standard KiCad geometry. No exposed pad (none on this package). The area under U1 on F.Cu is a copper pour with a via grid (image work/P01-stm32f407/u1_top.png). Y1 pads 2.0x2.4, pitch 3.7 mm vs YXC 1.7 wide, 3.7 pitch: fine.
Decoupling distance (centre of cap to pad): pin 100: C1 2.9; pin 75: C10(VCAP2) 2.8, C6 3.9; pin 50: C5 3.4; pin 19/21/22: C3 2.9-3.2; pin 11: C3 5.1; pin 28: C3 7.2 (nothing nearer); C4 7.5 from pin 50; C11/C12/FB1 about 8.4 from pins 21/22 (VDDA path). 6-layer board has In4 +3V3 plane and In1 GND so inductance is moderate; rev 0.9 4-layer ordered board has the same placement (positions identical in ord_db).

### 3c. Electrical stress
- Supply: rail +3V3 from LDL1117S33 with 3.56 V TL431/Q20 clamp: 3.56 V < 3.6 V operating, 4.0 V abs max, peak 3.93 V (repo documented, not re-reported).
- Inputs: all clamps go only to +3V3 (BAT54WS or 1N4148WS on pins), none to GND. FT pins (PE7/8/9-11, PC6/7) sit at about VDD+0.65 V max (3.9-4.0 V) under battery fault: within VDD+4. Analog pins in analog mode: pin held at +3V3+Vf (BAT54WS, repo documents 3.57-3.63 V at 24 V vs VDDA+0.3 for TTa PA4/PA5). PA4/PA5 (TPS, MAP) are the only TTa pins; they carry the same circuit as FT pins.
- PA8 open-drain with R71 10k to +5V: FT pin, 5 V level allowed when VDD >= 2 V; when 5 V rises before 3V3 the pin sees 5 V with VDD < 1 V (abs max VDD+4) only for the ramp time through 10k; low risk, not reported.
- PB3/PB4 reset state: JTDO floating / NJTRST pull-up, but both feed the B (3.3 V) side of U11 whose output enable pin 22 (powered by VCCA=5 V) is pulled to 5 V by R71 until firmware releases it: outputs high-Z. PA15 unused. PA13/PA14 stay SWD. OK.
- PC13/PC14 drive only CMOS inputs of U12 (no LED, no load): within 3 mA / 2 MHz rule. PC14 LSE disabled in mcuconf (comment cites RM0090). OK.
- LEDs: 1k from 3.3 V into 3.1 V (green/blue datasheet Vf at 5 mA) LEDs: current well below 1 mA, likely dim (INFERRED; LED inside a closed ECU case anyway).
- Boot: BOOT0 low by 10k unless SW1; BOOT1 low by 10k; correct per DS (BOOT0=0 -> flash). SW1 shorts +3V3 to BOOT0 through nothing but R1 10k to GND: 3.3 mA? no: 3.3 V/10k = 0.33 mA only while pressed. OK.
- Thermal: see summary 6.
