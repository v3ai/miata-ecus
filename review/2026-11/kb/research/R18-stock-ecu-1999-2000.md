# R18 - The stock 1999-2000 Miata (NB1) engine computer in depth

Status: DONE WITH GAPS (2026-11-16). Files: kb/research/files/R18/ (INDEX.md there). Scratch: work/R18-stock-ecu-1999-2000/.
Tags: VERIFIED = read today in primary source (Mazda manual images, read by eye; low-res contact-sheet reads are marked "low-res"), INFERRED, UNVERIFIED.

## Summary
1. The 1999-2001 Mazda manual (CHARM 1999 and scanned 1999-2001 FSM) has NO terminal-voltage table for the NB1 PCM; it only has a PID-to-PCM-terminal table (4 images saved). This is a primary terminal map for ~45 terminals. The 2000 wiring manual gives connector-view tables with wire colours for all three plugs (VERIFIED, images saved).
2. Primary data CONFLICTS with the repo's secondary-source pinout (repo/research/pinouts_9605.md) on: 1H (Mazda: B+2, constant battery via 30 A FUEL INJ fuse, wire G/R, repo: "unclear, test pad, no-drive" - no-drive is right but the meaning is now known); 3K (Mazda: fuel tank level signal FTL, wire BR/B; repo: "N/C vs fuel gauge, low"); 2K (Mazda wiring: wire G/O goes to cluster, DLC and TCM = tach output; Alex Engineering "unused" is wrong); 1D/1L (DLC: 1L = TEN terminal, 1D = K-line, low-res).
3. Plugs: 3 separate harness plugs 22 + 16 + 26 = 64 cavities, names 1A-1V, 2A-2P, 3A-3Z (letters I, O used, Q used; no letters skipped except as shown below). 2G, 1C, 1J, 1M, 3D, 3P, 3R unused on the US car (marked * in diagram).
4. Sensors: CKP and CMP are 3-wire Hall-type, 12 V fed from main-relay W/R feed (A = power, B = signal to PCM 2J / 2H, C = ground B/L). MAF is 3-wire (C = W/R 12 V, B = LG/B signal to 2L, A = B/L ground). Knock sensor 2-wire piezo, about 560 kohm sensor-to-body at 20 C, signal on 2F. EGR valve = 4-coil stepper (2M/2N/2O/2P low-side, W/R common feed on C and D) -- not a vacuum EGR. VICS solenoid 3Q low side. Coil packs: 2 packs (cyl 1-4, 2-3) with built-in igniter, 3 wires each (battery B via INJ fuse, ground B/W, IGT in BR/Y = 3G, BR = 3H).
5. Alternator: generator connector has D and P (plus stud B). PCM 1O = field-coil duty (ALTF PID; 0 % key on), 1T = generator output voltage sense (ALTT V: 0 V key on, B+ +1 V idle), 1Q = charge lamp driven by PCM (ON key on, OFF idle).
6. Immobiliser: no evidence of any on US 1999-2000. Only AMP/DIYAutoTune jumper "Europe w/ immob." (UNVERIFIED for markets). PCM 3P is the European fuel-pump-relay/immob circuit per DIY docs (UNVERIFIED).
7. Reflash: no commercial reflash/read-write tool for NB1 found (searches only returned standalone PnP ECUs); forum thread says nothing exists for earlier MX-5 (UNVERIFIED).
8. Not found: maker, teardown photos, case dimensions, board outline, TE part numbers beyond the rusEFI wiki. See Not checked.

## Connector (what is known)
- Three plugs on the harness: 22 (section 1), 16 (section 2), 26 (section 3) = 64 (VERIFIED by connector tables in 2000 wiring, images). Section 1 has 22 positions 1A-1V (no gap), section 2 has 2A-2P (16), section 3 has 3A-3Z minus none? the table shows 3A..3Z = 26. Good.
- rusEFI wiki OEM-connectors: board header TE 176122-6 or 4-174518-7 (64 pin: 22+16+26), harness plugs 22p 174515-6/175110-6, 16p 174514-6/175109-6, 26p 174516-6/175111-6 (UNVERIFIED; wiki, local copy files/R18/rusefi_wiki_OEM-connectors.md lines 317-342, 417-423; TE page not opened). Wiki also notes 26p = 10 high-current + 16 low-current, 22p = 6 high + 16 low, 16p all low.
- Plugs lock with clips; "fairly tight fit" (AMP install page, https://ampenginemanagement.com/blogs/mspnp-pro-documentation/pro-mm9900-installation).

## Location / mounting
Under the driver's dash beside the steering column, secured by two bolts, lower bolt visible, upper bolt on top of case, between brake and clutch pedals; AMP PnP is zip-tied to the lower bracket and clutch pedal bracket (AMP page above; VERIFIED text). Manual: PCM removal 01-40-3: remove one bolt and one nut (stud), tightening torque 7.9-10.7 N m (80-110 kgf-cm) (low-res contact sheet read, files/R18/sm1999-2001_p290-301_contact.png).

## Function notes
- CKP: 3-wire, supply from main relay W/R (terminal A), signal B -> 2J, ground C -> B/L (VERIFIED, 01-40-13 text low-res, wiring B-1b). Plate behind crank pulley with four projections, air gap 0.5-1.5 mm. CMP: A = W/R 12 V, B = 2H, C ground. CMP frequency at idle 750-850 rpm = 18-22 Hz; table 1000 rpm = 25 Hz, 2000 = 50 Hz, 3000 = 75 Hz (01-40-16). INFERRED: 25 Hz at 1000 rpm = 3 pulses per cam revolution (8.33 cam rev/s x 3). Hall type is INFERRED from the "threshold voltage 2 V / 6 V" meter setting, 12 V supply and open-circuit tests; rusEFI/Haltech say Hall (secondary).
- MAF: 3 terminals (A ground, B signal, C 12 V). Flow spec 2.6-8.3 g/s at idle MT (01-40-10, low-res).
- Knock sensor: present in the US PID table (KR, 2F) for 1999; market differences not checked.
- Heated O2: front (2C, heater 1U) and rear (3J, heater 3V); "except California" vs "California" connector variants differ (01-40-18). Narrowband zirconia (0-1 V).
- Fuel tank pressure sensor 2A, CDCV 3U, EGR boost sensor 3S/3T: US evaporative / OBD2 items.
- Idle: IAC solenoid on 3M with 12 V feed 3O.
- Alternator: generator terminals B, D, P. Standard voltage table (01-17-3): key on D about 0 V, P < 1 V; idle B 13-15 V, P approx 3-8 V. Charge lamp: key on ON, off after start; if no lamp, check lamp/harness/PCM 1Q. If the PCM does not drive 1O: not documented by Mazda; forum says charge lamp path supplies initial field current on some alternators (UNVERIFIED, thefactoryfiveforum/hpacademy via search). A Locost forum post says Mk2 alternators are ECU controlled (UNVERIFIED).
- OBD-II: manual uses DLC-2 with KLN terminal; K-line (ISO 9141 / 14230 family) INFERRED; not found as text. TEN = DLC terminal to PCM 1L.
- Cruise control: separate cruise control module (CCM), also selectable in NGS (PCM, ABS, CCM). Not in the PCM. (Manual 01-40-15 low-res; cruise PDF not read.)
- Tach 2K, speed 2D W/B (VSS to PCM), A/T: 1K/1N TCM link.
- Power steering pressure switch 1G, A/C: 1P switch, 1S relay, 1I condenser fan relay.

## Implications for this project
1. Motherboard 1999-2000 pin table: re-check against the Mazda tables. Concrete corrections: 1H must stay unconnected or fused (constant battery, G/R from FUEL INJ 30 A, not a signal), 3K is the fuel tank level signal to the PCM (an input; leaving it open on the ECU is harmless: fuel gauge itself is in the cluster, PCM only monitors) , 2K is tach. Evidence: PID table + wiring (files/R18). Confidence high for 1H/3K, medium for 2K. Effect: documentation / harness cavity naming in motherboard 1999-2000.
2. EGR valve is a stepper with 4 low-side coils on 2M-2P (W/R common via main relay). The project leaves EGR out; if the EGR is not driven the PCM that is gone anyway; with no EGR drive, the engine runs (rusEFI side). Document that unplugging is irrelevant. Confidence medium.
3. 12 V sensor supply: CKP, CMP and MAF get 12 V on W/R; rusEFI inputs must handle open-collector Hall outputs with pull-ups and the MAF analog 0.9-2.4 V (MAF 12 V powered, 5 V? signal not measured). Confidence medium.
4. Alternator: ships off; PCM-less alternator may not excite (field D line). Add a documentation warning that the battery lamp (1Q) path and field D (1O) are PCM-driven; test alternator D terminal "about 0 V key on". Confidence medium.
5. Placeholder outline: no primary dimensions found. The best we have: PCM bolts to a dash bracket with two fasteners; measure a stock ECU. Confidence high that outline still needs a physical measurement.
6. No immobiliser on US cars from any source found; Europe variant is the only suspect (3P). Document as "US only".
7. 1D is K-line to DLC: if the board does not drive it, the scan tool does nothing (known limitation).
