# Quick start: 1989–93 (NA6)

One page for this model-year range. The full guide is [INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md); each step below links to its section. **Nothing here has been run in a car yet.** Steps nobody has checked are marked unchecked in the guide.

Other years: [1989–93](1989-93.md) · [1994–95](1994-95.md) · [1996–97](1996-97.md) · [1999–2000](1999-2000.md) · [2001–05](2001-05.md)

Manual-gearbox cars (and automatics swapped to manual) are the supported ones. **Automatic cars are untested.**

## What is in the box

The ECU, assembled and flashed: the daughterboard soldered to the 1989–95 motherboard, which carries the MAP sensor, the wideband connector and USB-C. If you built your own, do [BUILD.md](../BUILD.md) first.

## What you add

- A **Bosch LSU 4.9** wideband sensor (0 258 017 025), genuine, and **AEM's X-Series sensor harness 30-3427** (or a lead made up to the J2 pin map).
- **Vacuum hose**, 3–4 mm inside diameter, about 3 m, and a **4 mm barbed tee**.
- A USB-C cable and a laptop with [TunerStudio](https://www.tunerstudio.com/).
- A multimeter and a timing light.
- Optional, manual cars: the throttle position sensor kit ([README](../README.md#throttle-position-sensor-198993-manual-cars)).

Everything is on the [parts page](../additional-parts/shopping_list.html).

## Where the stock ECU is

1990–93: passenger footwell, under the carpet, behind a metal cover at the front of the footwell. Held by four nuts and a bolt, which also hold the cover. (The location table in the guide starts at 1990.)

Keep it: its case is the new ECU's case, and its board is your way back to stock. The 1989–95 board has the stock NA case outline and six M4 mounting holes.

## Gotchas for this year

- **Leave the airflow meter plugged in.** Its air-temperature sensor is the one the ECU reads, and its pump contact keeps switching the fuel pump through the circuit opening relay, as with the stock ECU. The ECU ignores the meter's airflow signal. To take the meter out, see [Taking the airflow meter out](../INSTALL.md#taking-the-airflow-meter-out-optional): that changes JP6 and the pump wiring.
- **Manual cars have no throttle position sensor**, only a switch. The engine runs, but there is no closed-loop idle, no throttle-based acceleration fuel and no overrun fuel cut until you fit one ([how](../README.md#throttle-position-sensor-198993-manual-cars)).
- **Automatics (untested):** never fit R39, and leave R28 and R30 off.
- **Injectors:** the stock harness joins them in pairs on 2U and 2V, so the ECU fires them in pairs. For sustained high rpm in a hot cabin, either set the injection mode to Sequential or convert the car to one wire per injector (both unchecked): [year-specific steps](../INSTALL.md#4-year-specific-steps) and [the conversion](../INSTALL.md#one-wire-per-injector-on-a-199093-car-optional). Measure one injector's inductance first.
- **1993 California cars** may already have one wire per injector. Check cavities 2Y and 2Z against injector plugs 3 and 4 before the first start ([how](../INSTALL.md#4-year-specific-steps)).
- Add-ons (boost valve, flex fuel, nitrous, CAN): [section 9](../INSTALL.md#9-add-ons-on-the-add-on-header). On the motherboard they are J5 pins.

## Air temperature and MAP hose

The stock sensor is inside the airflow meter. ECU cavities: signal 2P, ground 2D.

**MAP hose:** take vacuum from the small nipple on the intake manifold just behind the throttle body. With cruise control that nipple already feeds the actuator: cut that hose and fit the tee. Through the firewall by the rubber plug behind the windshield-washer bottle: drill a hole in it just big enough for the hose. If the reading jumps around, tee into the fuel pressure regulator's hose at the back of the manifold instead. Never take it from before the throttle plate, the brake booster or the crankcase breather.

## First-start checks

The full list: [INSTALL.md, First start](../INSTALL.md#8-first-start) and [firmware/README.md, First start](../firmware/README.md#first-start).

1. Key on, engine off: green lights on, no red light. TunerStudio connects. Coolant and air temperature read the weather, MAP reads about 100 kPa, battery reads within half a volt of a meter on the battery.
2. Air temperature, engine cold: within a few degrees of coolant temperature. About -40 °C means an open circuit (an unplugged meter or sensor).
3. Throttle: calibrate closed and wide open (not on a manual car without a sensor).
4. Crank: the fuel pump primes, RPM shows about 200, and the engine starts.
5. Base timing: lock it at 10°, check with a timing light on cylinder 1, correct "Trigger Angle Advance" until the light agrees, then unlock it.
6. Warm it up and watch the wideband: lambda far from 1.0 at idle means stop and find out why. First drives: light throttle only.
