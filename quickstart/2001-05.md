# Quick start: 2001–05 (NB2, VVT)

One page for this model-year range. The full guide is [INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md); each step below links to its section. **Nothing here has been run in a car yet.** Steps nobody has checked are marked unchecked in the guide.

Other years: [1989–93](1989-93.md) · [1994–95](1994-95.md) · [1996–97](1996-97.md) · [1999–2000](1999-2000.md) · [2001–05](2001-05.md)

Manual-gearbox cars (and automatics swapped to manual) are the supported ones. **Automatic cars are untested.**

## What is in the box

The ECU, assembled and flashed: the daughterboard soldered to the 2001–05 motherboard, which carries the MAP sensor, the wideband connector and USB-C. If you built your own, do [BUILD.md](../BUILD.md) first.

## What you add

- A **Bosch LSU 4.9** wideband sensor (0 258 017 025), genuine, and **AEM's X-Series sensor harness 30-3427** (or a lead made up to the J2 pin map).
- **Vacuum hose**, 3–4 mm inside diameter, about 3 m, and a **4 mm barbed tee**.
- A USB-C cable and a laptop with [TunerStudio](https://www.tunerstudio.com/).
- A multimeter and a timing light.

Everything is on the [parts page](../additional-parts/shopping_list.html).

## Where the stock ECU is

Under the dash on the driver's side, next to the steering column. Held by two 10 mm nuts; the upper one is out of sight.

Keep it: its case is the new ECU's case, and its board is your way back to stock. **The 1999–2005 case fit is unchecked.** The board has the 1990–95 outline and six M4 holes as a stand-in. Check the fit against your case before cutting anything.

## Settings for this year

Year button: **2001-02 or 2003-05. They are separate buttons.** There are no jumpers on this board. Harness header: TE 353830-5, 72 positions.

## Gotchas for this year

- **Pick the right year button.** A 2001–02 car on the 2003–05 button gets no fuel or spark.
- 2003–05: the ECU holds the main relay (the 2001–02 does not).
- **Charging:** alternator control ships switched off until the wire has been checked on a car. Check the wire and switch it on ([firmware/README.md, First start, step 7](../firmware/README.md#first-start)). Until then, watch the battery voltage.
- The airflow meter can stay plugged in or come out: the ECU ignores its signal. The air-temperature sensor is a separate two-wire sensor in the air filter box, and the ECU reads it as it is.
- The knock sensor is the stock one. Variable valve timing is run closed loop from rusEFI's target table. The stock oxygen-sensor heater is not driven.
- **Mazdaspeed (2004–05) is not supported.** Its pin 4R is a second air-temperature sensor, not the VVT valve.
- A car with an immobiliser may flash its security light: the ECU does not talk to it.
- Add-ons (boost valve, flex fuel, nitrous, CAN): [section 9](../INSTALL.md#9-add-ons-on-the-add-on-header). On the motherboard they are J5 pins.

## Air temperature and MAP hose

The stock sensor is a separate two-wire sensor in the air filter box. ECU cavities: signal 4N, ground 4O.

**MAP hose:** take vacuum from the nipple on top of the intake manifold. Through the firewall by the large rubber grommet in the driver's-side corner: slit it carefully, it is full of wires. Never take it from before the throttle plate, the brake booster or the crankcase breather.

## First-start checks

The full list: [INSTALL.md, First start](../INSTALL.md#8-first-start) and [firmware/README.md, First start](../firmware/README.md#first-start).

1. Key on, engine off: green lights on, no red light. TunerStudio connects. Coolant and air temperature read the weather, MAP reads about 100 kPa, battery reads within half a volt of a meter on the battery.
2. Air temperature, engine cold: within a few degrees of coolant temperature. About -40 °C means an open circuit (an unplugged meter or sensor).
3. Throttle: calibrate closed and wide open.
4. Crank: the fuel pump primes, RPM shows about 200, and the engine starts.
5. Base timing: lock it at 10°, check with a timing light on cylinder 1, correct "Trigger Angle Advance" until the light agrees, then unlock it.
6. Warm it up and watch the wideband: lambda far from 1.0 at idle means stop and find out why. First drives: light throttle only.
7. On a 2001–05, no fuel or spark with the key on usually means the wrong year button (2001–02 against 2003–05).
